# Discovery cancellation and paced inventory

## Evidence and limits

The live attempt started after the local 16:44:45.399 UTC pre-status and returned by 16:44:50.755 UTC post-status. Six accepted B2 status samples recorded heartbeat ages 0.578–0.942 s and retained HOLD, DISARMED, inhibit, STOP latch and TX disabled. These are sampled ages, not heartbeat packet intervals. PWM was reported fresh at 999/999 immediately before the attempt. A later sample showed 999/999 with age 0.890 s. The failing assertion was exactly servo_state == FRESH AND SERVO5 == 999 AND SERVO6 == 999. Its input snapshot was not retained. Inter-frame PWM timing before or during the attempt, the Servo values at failure, per-frame heartbeat cadence, process CPU, RX queue depth and IPC latency cannot be reconstructed from those artifacts. The old postboot report is a different interval and cannot establish PWM rate for this attempt.

The external observer revoked the discovery marker. The old Owner checks the guard again while a sent request is pending and latches transport_fault on abort. The generic result and timing are consistent with that path. They do not distinguish it from every other in-flight fault path, and there is no recorded reason suggesting a serial disconnect.

## Plausible load mechanism

Rover PARAM_REQUEST_LIST can stream batches subject to a firmware link budget. The Pi reader consumes up to 4096 bytes and parses a batch in serial wire order. Each frame calls diagnostics, operator and log observer synchronously. Transaction work and journal fsync run off this loop. The Owner tick handles safety and priority work before issuing a request, but that ordering cannot preempt firmware TX or skip ahead of queued bytes. A parameter burst could delay SERVO_OUTPUT_RAW at the firmware, driver or Pi dispatch layer. CPU contention or IPC timing could contribute. None of these mechanisms is confirmed by the retained live evidence. [Rover parameter stream](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ArduPilot/ardupilot/Rover-4.7.2/libraries/GCS_MAVLink/GCS_Param.cpp#L36-L90)

## Replacement and verification

Read index 0 to retrieve param_count, then explicitly read every index and reject inconsistent metadata or a gap. Return only the LOG_DISARMED proof plus numeric counters. Re-read its numerical index independently once the scan completed and the stream is quiet. An indexed reply carries the requested index and parameter count, unlike a named read or write echo with sentinel 65535. One outstanding request at most and 100 ms between scan sends control traffic. A 4096-position scan takes at least about 410 seconds at the maximum pace, with a hard cap of 480 seconds plus no further wire requests after the cap. A device that cannot complete within the cap yields INCONCLUSIVE. At 10 pairs/s the nominal unsigned MAVLink 2 traffic is approximately 0.6 kB/s before other telemetry. This is a model, not a measurement. [Rover indexed handler](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ArduPilot/ardupilot/Rover-4.7.2/libraries/GCS_MAVLink/GCS_Param.cpp#L355-L388)

Keep the current PWM freshness limit below 3 s and 999/999 requirement. Enforce both inside discovery at the Owner and retain one diagnostic snapshot on failure. No receive ordering change, message rate command, writes or guard relaxation. Priority means the new sender defers to the existing priority gate and safety checks. The synthetic flood test verifies bounded collection and continued dispatch of another critical-clock message, not firmware scheduling or measured vehicle telemetry latency.
